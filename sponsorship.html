Format

The engagement sandwich

The show is deliberately wrapped in networking on both sides, so sponsors get time with attendees directly rather than just a logo on a slide.

  1. 30–60 min Networking Doors, open bar, themed drinks with your branding on the menu
  2. 75 min Run of show Professional comedians who work in tech — Fortune 500 CEOs, senior technical staff, sales, product and growth
  3. Ad nauseum More networking The part where your pipeline actually happens
